About.

CascadAI is a project to automatically determine the score of a single player's Cascadia board game from a photograph of the completed environment.

Project

The system detects the animal tokens on the board from an uploaded image, builds their adjacency graph, and applies the rules for each selected scoring card (A, B, C, or D) to compute the final score.

Scoring cards are selected per animal type on the Auto Score page.
